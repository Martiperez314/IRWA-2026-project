Query length

Words per query. Short queries (1–2 words) are ambiguous and return many results; long ones risk 0 results with the AND search, which is why the fallback exists.

Queries with no results, or answered only with partial matches (fallback). They point to missing products, typos or vocabulary the index does not know.
